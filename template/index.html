Cert Study Trackers

Interactive checklists for professional certifications. Every item traces to the certifying body's own exam guide or requirements page, and every tracker says how far it has been validated.

Tick items as you go. Progress is saved in your browser only; nothing is sent anywhere.

How much to trust each tracker

  • Field-validatedThe author passed this certification and checked the list against that experience.
  • Source-tracedBuilt from the official sources and not yet tested by an exam attempt. A structured reading of the blueprint.
  • Peer-reviewedBuilt from official sources and reviewed by someone who holds the certification.

Not affiliated with or endorsed by any certification body. Certification names are trademarks of their owners. These trackers contain no exam questions or exam content; where a tracker and the official guide disagree, the guide wins.
