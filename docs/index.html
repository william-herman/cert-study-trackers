How much to trust each tracker

  • Field-validatedThe author passed this certification and checked the list against that experience.
  • Source-tracedBuilt from the official sources and not yet tested by an exam attempt. A structured reading of the blueprint.
  • Peer-reviewedBuilt from official sources and reviewed by someone who holds the certification.

Hold one of these certifications? Review its tracker

Reviews from people who passed are what move a tracker to Peer-reviewed. It takes about five minutes.

What we'll ask

  • Which tracker, whether you hold the certification, and when you passed
  • How well the tracker matches the exam
  • What's wrong, outdated or missing
  • What the exam emphasized (optional; topics and patterns only)
  • Whether you'd like credit by name

Never share actual exam questions or answers. Exams are under NDA, and reviews containing exam content are deleted.

Opens the review form on GitHub in a new tab. You'll need a free GitHub account, and reviews are public.
